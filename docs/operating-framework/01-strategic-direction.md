## Mission

**DECIDED** — Noetia exists so that knowledge is not only consumed but **expressed**,
turning reading into a social identity behavior ([`PRD.md`](../PRD.md)).

## Vision

**DECIDED** — to become the leading platform for that behavior: read, listen, capture,
share, as one continuous act rather than four separate tools.

## Positioning

**DECIDED** — a **distribution and reading platform**, not a publisher. The catalog is
built by authors, publishers, and companies. This is the anchor anti-goal of the
[Project Charter](00-project-charter.md) and shapes every downstream decision: creator
economics are first-class, catalog growth is a supply-chain problem, and Noetia never
competes with its own suppliers for content.

## Differentiation

**DECIDED** — three claims, in the order the product makes them:

1. **Phrase-level synchronized reading and listening** (*Escucha Activa*) — not an
   audiobook with a text mode, but one experience with two surfaces.
2. **Frictionless capture** — fragments as a first-class object, not an afterthought.
3. **Instant social expression** — branded quote cards as the output of reading.

A fourth, economic, claim is aimed at creators: a **45% combined creator share** for
self-narrated titles, positioned against Audible's 25–40%
([`01-business-plan.md:429`](../business/en/01-business-plan.md)). Per
[PO-007](decisions/product-owner/PO-007-canonical-revenue-allocation.md) this figure is
valid **only** for the self-narrated case and must always name whose share it is.

## Markets and language

**DECIDED (emphasis) · OPEN (boundary)**

Documented strategic emphasis:

- **Latin America**
- **Spanish-speaking users**
- **Hispanics in the United States**
- **Bilingual ES/EN capability** — implemented today: the catalog carries both Spanish and
  English titles, the web and mobile apps ship full ES/EN i18n with enforced parity, and
  ADR-004 requires multilingual (including cross-language) embeddings.

Market evidence cited: Spanish-language audiobooks grew 45.7% in 2023, with Latin America
+9% and Mexico +12% (Bookwire, Spanish Market Report 2024).

`OPEN — Q-13:` whether this emphasis is a **priority** or a **boundary** has never been
decided. NOF-001 §10 explicitly forbids assuming it excludes future markets. The framework
records emphasis without inferring exclusion.

## Target audiences

**INFERRED** from the product hierarchy and business plan; no formal segmentation document
exists:

| Audience | Role |
|---|---|
| Readers | Daily active users — the product's centre of gravity |
| Authors, publishers, companies | Content supply; occasional but business-critical interaction |
| Narrators | Distinct economic participant (PO-007), may or may not be the author |

## Long-term ambition

**PARTIALLY DECIDED** — Noetia+ extends the platform from reading into **knowledge work**:
grounded Q&A over owned books, highlights, and notes, with provenance
(PO-002 / PO-003). Designed in detail (25 documents), not implemented.

The ambition beyond Noetia+ is not recorded.
