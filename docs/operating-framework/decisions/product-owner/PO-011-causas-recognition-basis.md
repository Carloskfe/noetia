# PO-011 — Causas Noetia Recognition Basis

**Status:** `DECIDED` · **Domain:** Economics, Social Impact · **Authority:** Product Owner
**Recorded by:** NEM-007 Phase A
**Resolves:** [C-11](../../governance/contradictions-and-gaps.md) at the policy level · Q-15
`ACCOUNTING REVIEW REQUIRED`

---

## Decision

**Causas Noetia's 2.22% allocation arises from qualifying collected revenue, not from
subsequent book redemption.**

The commitment therefore does **not** disappear when:

- a token remains unused;
- a paid token expires;
- redemption occurs later, or never.

Causas is a **payment-level** allocation — a social-impact commitment attached to money
Noetia actually collected.

## What this resolves

[NOF-002](../../analysis/creator-economics-stress-test.md) §7 found the business plan
promising "2.22% of **every payment**" while [PO-007](PO-007-canonical-revenue-allocation.md)
allocated per **qualifying redemption**. Modelled at 10,000 subscribers with 75% redemption,
the two bases funded Causas at **$30,633/yr versus $22,975/yr** — a 25% gap that widened as
redemption fell.

PO-011 settles it on the **payment** basis, which is also the basis the public commitment
already states. The public claim and the internal rule now agree.

## Relationship to PO-007

PO-007's percentages are unchanged. What PO-011 establishes is **when** the Causas
allocation arises. The 2.22% is computed against qualifying collected revenue at payment;
it does not wait for, and is not contingent on, a redemption.

This makes Causas structurally different from the creator allocations, which
[PO-012](PO-012-marketing-and-breakage-treatment.md) confirms arise only at qualifying
redemption.

## Review boundary

`ACCOUNTING REVIEW REQUIRED` for recognition, refunds, reversals, taxation, donation
mechanics, and reporting. PO-011 decides the **product/business basis**; it is not an
accounting determination and does not establish how the allocation is recognized, held, or
disbursed.
