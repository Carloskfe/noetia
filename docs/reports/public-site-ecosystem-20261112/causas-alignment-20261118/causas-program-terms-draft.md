## Causas Noetia — Términos del programa (borrador)

### 1. Qué es Causas Noetia

Causas Noetia es un programa planificado mediante el cual Noetia destinará una parte de sus propios ingresos a organizaciones benéficas independientes. Las causas no son operadas por Noetia.

El programa aún no ha comenzado. Estos términos describen cómo Noetia pretende operarlo cuando comience.

### 2. Cuánto se destina

Noetia destinará el 2,22% de los ingresos netos elegibles.

- "Ingresos netos elegibles" significa el importe que Noetia efectivamente recibe por un pago elegible, después de las comisiones de procesamiento de pago.

- [Pendiente de definir: el tratamiento de los impuestos cobrados.]

### 3. Tu elección de causa

Puedes elegir una de las tres causas:

- Bienestar animal
- Niñez y juventud
- Medio ambiente

Si eliges una causa, el 100% de la asignación de tus pagos elegibles se destina a esa causa. Si no eliges ninguna, la asignación se reparte por igual entre las tres. No hay otras opciones de distribución.

Las asignaciones se calcularán y liquidarán mensualmente.

- Si un pago se reembolsa después de la liquidación mensual, la contribución ya entregada no se revierte: Noetia asume ese costo y tu reembolso no se reduce por ello.

## Causas Noetia — Program terms (draft)

### 1. What Causas Noetia is

Causas Noetia is a planned program through which Noetia will allocate part of its own receipts to independent charitable organizations. The causes are not operated by Noetia.

The program has not started. These terms describe how Noetia intends to operate it once it starts.

[To be confirmed by legal counsel: your payment is the price of a Noetia service and is not a donation by you to any organization.]

### 2. How much is allocated

Noetia will allocate 2.22% of eligible net receipts.

- "Eligible net receipts" means the amount Noetia actually receives for an eligible payment, after payment-processing fees.
- [To be defined: which payments are eligible (subscriptions, tokens, gifts or others).]
- [To be defined: how collected taxes are treated.]

### 3. Your choice of cause

You can choose one of three causes:

- Animal welfare
- Children and youth
- Environment

If you choose a cause, 100% of the allocation from your eligible payments goes to that cause. If you don't choose one, the allocation is split equally among all three. There are no other distribution options.

[To be defined: from when a change of cause applies.]

### 4. Monthly calculation and settlement

Allocations will be calculated and settled monthly.

[To be defined: cut-off dates and settlement timing.]

### 5. Refunds

- A payment refunded before the monthly settlement is excluded from the allocation.
- If a payment is refunded after the monthly settlement, the contribution already disbursed is not reversed: Noetia absorbs that cost, and your refund is not reduced because of it.
